One simple classroom flow

  1. Prepare. Teacher uploads optional course context and case material separately. XIPU returns editable case and discussion-design proposals.
  2. Review and open groups. Teacher reviews nine settings together, checks the student preview, publishes and shares join codes. Students join with pseudonyms.
  3. Discuss. Teacher starts the activity. Students chat and may invite one AI role. No extra goal or group-submission form is needed.
  4. Reflect. Teacher ends and freezes the discussion. Students submit one reflection before viewing feedback.
  5. Publish the outcome. XIPU drafts a group synthesis from the frozen chat. Teacher checks and publishes it. Students see the read-only poster and message-count chart; the overview shows each group's outcome.

Message counts describe participation, not ability or a grade. Original contributions are retained. Optional detailed teacher assessments stay outside the primary student flow.

A generated draft is not automatically published. A network error is not a generated result. The static preview is a labelled simulation.
